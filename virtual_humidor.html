The Th3rd Rule.

Gray Hogan’s personal cigar, blended by Karen Berger — the “Queen of Cigars.” Bold, complex, and built to stand out in any humidor. Born here, now carried by select shops across Pennsylvania.

Wrapper
AJ Fernandez Cameroon
Binder
Nicaraguan Oscuro
Filler
Double Ligero · Estelí, NIC
Formats
Singles & Bundles

Named after The Tobacco Center’s 3rd Lounge Rule — this is not a house cigar that exists to fill a shelf. It’s the result of a real collaboration between Navy Veteran Gray Hogan and Karen Berger at her factory in Estelí, Nicaragua. Headlined by a rare AJ Fernandez Cameroon wrapper, a rich Nicaraguan Oscuro binder, and a potent double ligero filler. Bold enough to stand out. Not for the timid.
