A Real Collaboration, Not a House Brand.

The Third Rule isn't a cigar that exists to fill a shelf. It's the result of a real partnership between Navy Veteran Gray Hogan, owner of The Tobacco Center, and one of the most respected blenders in the industry.

Gray brought a precise vision — a cigar that reflected the discipline and standards of his Navy background, bold enough to stand out, balanced enough to reward the patient smoker. He took that vision to Karen Berger at her factory in Estelí, Nicaragua, and together they built something worth talking about.

"Developing 'The Third Rule' was an exercise in precision. I wanted a cigar that reflected the discipline and standards of my Navy background while offering a profile that truly stands out in a humidor. Having Karen Berger bring those ideas to life at her factory was an incredible experience." — Gray Hogan, Owner & Navy Veteran, The Tobacco Center

Named after The Tobacco Center's 3rd Lounge Rule, the cigar started as a shop exclusive and built a genuine following before ever leaving Williamsport. It's since grown beyond our humidor — now wholesaled to select tobacco shops across the state.
