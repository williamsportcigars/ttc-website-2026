# Bylaws of The Tobacco Center, Inc.

**DRAFT — for review by counsel before adoption. Intended to be adopted by the initial Board of Directors as part of the Organizational Consent following the effective date of the conversion.**

---

## Article I — Offices

**1.1 Registered Office.** The registered office of the corporation shall be as designated in the Articles of Incorporation, or as the Board of Directors may from time to time determine.

**1.2 Other Offices.** The corporation may also have offices at such other places as the Board of Directors may designate.

## Article II — Shareholders

**2.1 Annual Meeting.** An annual meeting of shareholders shall be held each year for the election of directors and the transaction of other business, at a date, time, and place (or by means of remote communication) fixed by the Board of Directors.

**2.2 Special Meetings.** Special meetings of shareholders may be called by the President, by a majority of the Board of Directors, or by shareholders holding at least [10]% of the outstanding shares entitled to vote.

**2.3 Notice.** Written notice of every shareholders' meeting, stating the place, date, time, and purpose(s), shall be given to each shareholder of record entitled to vote at least [10] and not more than [60] days before the meeting.

**2.4 Quorum.** The presence, in person or by proxy, of shareholders holding a majority of the shares entitled to vote shall constitute a quorum.

**2.5 Voting.** Each outstanding share of common stock shall be entitled to one vote on each matter submitted to a vote at a meeting of shareholders, except as otherwise provided by the Articles of Incorporation.

**2.6 Action Without a Meeting.** Any action required or permitted to be taken at a shareholders' meeting may be taken without a meeting if consented to in writing by shareholders holding at least the minimum number of votes that would be necessary to authorize the action at a meeting at which all shares entitled to vote were present and voted.

## Article III — Board of Directors

**3.1 Number.** The Board of Directors shall consist of [ONE / NUMBER] director(s), which number may be changed by resolution of the Board or the shareholders.

**3.2 Election and Term.** Directors shall be elected at each annual meeting of shareholders and shall hold office until the next annual meeting and until a successor is elected and qualified.

**3.3 Regular and Special Meetings.** The Board may hold regular meetings at times and places it determines, and special meetings may be called by the President or any director on reasonable notice to the other directors.

**3.4 Quorum and Voting.** A majority of the directors in office shall constitute a quorum. Except as otherwise required by law or the Articles of Incorporation, the vote of a majority of directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present shall be the act of the Board.

**3.5 Action Without a Meeting.** Any action required or permitted to be taken at a Board meeting may be taken without a meeting if consented to in writing by all directors.

**3.6 Vacancies.** Vacancies on the Board may be filled by a majority of the remaining directors, though less than a quorum.

**3.7 Removal.** Any director may be removed, with or without cause, by a vote of shareholders holding a majority of the shares entitled to vote.

## Article IV — Officers

**4.1 Designated Officers.** The officers of the corporation shall be a President, a Secretary, and a Treasurer, and such other officers as the Board may from time to time elect. One person may hold more than one office, except that the same person may not simultaneously hold the offices of President and Secretary.

**4.2 Election and Term.** Officers shall be elected annually by the Board of Directors and shall hold office until a successor is elected, or until earlier resignation or removal.

**4.3 President.** The President shall be the chief executive officer of the corporation, shall preside at meetings of shareholders and directors, and shall have general supervision of the corporation's business, subject to the direction of the Board.

**4.4 Secretary.** The Secretary shall keep the minutes of all meetings of shareholders and directors, give all notices required by law or these Bylaws, and have custody of the corporate records.

**4.5 Treasurer.** The Treasurer shall have custody of the corporation's funds and securities, keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements, and perform such other duties as the Board may assign.

**4.6 Removal.** Any officer may be removed, with or without cause, by the Board of Directors.

## Article V — Stock

**5.1 Certificates.** Shares of the corporation may be represented by certificates in such form as the Board approves, signed by the President and the Secretary (or Treasurer), or the corporation may issue shares in uncertificated form as the Board determines.

**5.2 Transfer.** Transfers of shares shall be made on the books of the corporation only upon surrender of the certificate(s) (if certificated) properly endorsed, or upon proper instruction for uncertificated shares.

**5.3 Lost Certificates.** The Board may authorize the issuance of a new certificate in place of one alleged to have been lost or destroyed, upon such terms (including indemnification of the corporation) as the Board requires.

## Article VI — Dividends

The Board of Directors may declare and the corporation may pay dividends on its outstanding shares, in cash, property, or shares of the corporation, subject to the restrictions of the Articles of Incorporation and applicable law.

## Article VII — Fiscal Year

The fiscal year of the corporation shall end on [DECEMBER 31 / OTHER DATE], unless otherwise determined by the Board.

## Article VIII — Indemnification

The corporation shall indemnify, to the fullest extent permitted by the Pennsylvania Business Corporation Law, any person who is or was a director or officer of the corporation, against liability incurred in connection with any proceeding by reason of serving in that capacity, except where the conduct is determined to constitute self-dealing, willful misconduct, or recklessness.

## Article IX — Amendments

These Bylaws may be amended or repealed, and new Bylaws adopted, by the Board of Directors or by the shareholders, as provided by the Articles of Incorporation and applicable law.
