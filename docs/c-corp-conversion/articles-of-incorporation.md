In compliance with the requirements of 15 Pa.C.S. § 1306 (relating to articles of incorporation) and the applicable provisions governing entity conversions, the undersigned corporation, desiring to be organized as a business corporation, hereby states that:

### Article 1 — Name

The name of the corporation is **The Tobacco Center, Inc.** *(Confirm availability via PA Dept. of State business entity name search before filing.)*

### Article 2 — Registered Office

The location and post office address of the corporation's registered office in Pennsylvania is:

[STREET ADDRESS], Williamsport, PA [ZIP CODE]

*(Must be a physical street address; a PO box alone is not sufficient as a registered office.)*

### Article 3 — Purpose

The corporation is incorporated under the Business Corporation Law of 1988, as amended, for the purpose of engaging in any lawful act or activity for which corporations may be organized under that law, including but not limited to the retail and wholesale sale of cigars and other tobacco products and related merchandise.

### Article 4 — Authorized Shares

The aggregate number of shares which the corporation shall have authority to issue is **1,000 shares of common stock, without par value**. *(Confirm this share count and par-value structure with the accountant — it should be set with the conversion ratio in the Plan of Conversion, and with any eventual QSBS basis considerations, in mind.)*

### Article 5 — Incorporator(s)

The name and mailing address of the incorporator(s) is/are:

[INCORPORATOR NAME], [ADDRESS]

### Article 6 — Term

The corporation shall have perpetual existence.

### Article 7 — Preemptive Rights

Shareholders of the corporation shall not have preemptive rights to acquire additional or treasury shares of the corporation. *(This is the Pennsylvania statutory default; state affirmatively here for clarity. Revisit if a future investor negotiates otherwise.)*

### Article 8 — Cumulative Voting

Shareholders shall not have the right to cumulate their votes for the election of directors. *(Also the Pennsylvania statutory default; state affirmatively for clarity.)*

### Article 9 — Director Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by the Pennsylvania Business Corporation Law as in effect or as it may be amended, a director of the corporation shall not be personally liable for monetary damages for any action taken, or any failure to take any action, unless the director has breached or failed to perform the duties of office and the breach or failure constitutes self-dealing, willful misconduct, or recklessness.

### Article 10 — Statement of Conversion

These Articles of Incorporation are being filed in connection with, and are effective as of, the conversion of The Tobacco Center, LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company, into this corporation pursuant to 15 Pa.C.S. § 361 et seq. and the Plan of Conversion adopted by the members of The Tobacco Center, LLC.
