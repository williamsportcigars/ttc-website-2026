(entity conversions), the undersigned, being [all of the members / members holding the requisite percentage per the Operating Agreement] of **The Tobacco Center, LLC**, a Pennsylvania limited liability company (the "Converting Entity"), hereby adopt the following Plan of Conversion:

### 1. Converting Entity

The Tobacco Center, LLC, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, PA entity number [ENTITY NUMBER], with a registered office at [CURRENT REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS].

### 2. Resulting Entity

**The Tobacco Center, Inc.**, a business corporation to be organized under the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania (the "Resulting Entity"), with Articles of Incorporation in the form attached to this Plan as Exhibit A.

### 3. Terms and Conditions of the Conversion

3.1. Upon the effective time of the conversion (the "Effective Time"), the Converting Entity shall be converted into the Resulting Entity, and the Resulting Entity shall, for all purposes of the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, be deemed to be a continuation of the existence of the Converting Entity in the resulting organizational form.

3.2. At the Effective Time, all of the Converting Entity's property, rights, privileges, liabilities, and obligations shall vest in and continue to be the property, rights, privileges, liabilities, and obligations of the Resulting Entity, without further act or deed.

3.3. At the Effective Time, each outstanding membership interest in the Converting Entity shall be converted into shares of common stock of the Resulting Entity on the following basis:

3.5. The officers of the Resulting Entity are authorized to take all actions necessary to effect the conversion, including filing the Statement of Conversion, Docketing Statement, and Articles of Incorporation with the Pennsylvania Department of State.
