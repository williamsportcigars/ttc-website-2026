# Organizational Consent of the Board of Directors

**The Tobacco Center, Inc.**

**DRAFT — for review by counsel and the accountant before signature. This is the board's first official action following the effective date of the conversion — it adopts the bylaws, issues stock to the former LLC members, appoints officers, and authorizes the practical steps (bank account, EIN application) needed to start operating as a corporation.**

---

The undersigned, being all of the directors of **The Tobacco Center, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation (the "Corporation"), formed by conversion of The Tobacco Center, LLC effective [EFFECTIVE DATE], hereby consent in writing, in lieu of an organizational meeting, to the adoption of the following resolutions, pursuant to 15 Pa.C.S. § 1727 (or successor provision) and the Corporation's Bylaws:

### 1. Ratification of Conversion and Filings

**RESOLVED**, that the Board ratifies and confirms the conversion of The Tobacco Center, LLC into the Corporation, the filing of the Statement of Conversion, Docketing Statement, and Articles of Incorporation with the Pennsylvania Department of State, and all actions taken by the incorporator(s) in connection therewith.

### 2. Adoption of Bylaws

**RESOLVED**, that the Bylaws presented to the Board (attached as Exhibit A) are hereby adopted as the Bylaws of the Corporation.

### 3. Issuance of Stock

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation shall issue shares of its common stock to the former members of The Tobacco Center, LLC in exchange for their membership interests, in accordance with the conversion ratio set forth in the Plan of Conversion, as follows:

| Shareholder | Shares issued | Consideration |
|---|---|---|
| [MEMBER NAME 1] | [NUMBER] | Membership interest in The Tobacco Center, LLC, contributed in connection with the conversion |
| [MEMBER NAME 2] | [NUMBER] | Membership interest in The Tobacco Center, LLC, contributed in connection with the conversion |

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that the officers are authorized to issue stock certificates (or uncertificated book-entry positions, as the Board determines) evidencing the above share issuances, and that the Corporation's stock ledger be updated accordingly.

**FURTHER RESOLVED**, that it is the intent of the Board and the shareholders that this issuance qualify as a tax-free exchange under Section 351 of the Internal Revenue Code, and that the Corporation's accountant be directed to confirm this treatment and prepare any required tax filings or disclosures.

### 4. Appointment of Officers

**RESOLVED**, that the following persons are appointed to the offices set forth opposite their names, to serve until their successors are duly elected and qualified:

| Office | Name |
|---|---|
| President | [NAME] |
| Secretary | [NAME] |
| Treasurer | [NAME] |

### 5. Fiscal Year

**RESOLVED**, that the fiscal year of the Corporation shall end on [DECEMBER 31 / OTHER DATE].

### 6. Federal Tax Matters — EIN and Classification

**RESOLVED**, that the officers are authorized to apply for a new federal Employer Identification Number for the Corporation (IRS Form SS-4), and to make such federal tax elections or filings as the Corporation's accountant advises are necessary or appropriate, including confirming the Corporation's classification as a C-corporation for federal income tax purposes.

### 7. State Tax Registration

**RESOLVED**, that the officers are authorized to register the Corporation with the Pennsylvania Department of Revenue (Form PA-100) for sales tax, employer withholding, and any other applicable tax accounts under the Corporation's new EIN, and to take all actions necessary to transition the Corporation's tax filings from the pass-through filings previously made by The Tobacco Center, LLC.

### 8. Licenses and Permits

**RESOLVED**, that the officers are authorized and directed to contact the Pennsylvania Department of Revenue's tobacco licensing division, and any other licensing authority as applicable, to transfer or reissue in the Corporation's name all licenses and permits previously held by The Tobacco Center, LLC, including without limitation its Other Tobacco Products license, before conducting business or applying for any additional state licenses under the Corporation's name.

### 9. Banking

**RESOLVED**, that the officers are authorized to open one or more deposit accounts for the Corporation at a bank or banks of their choosing, using the Corporation's new EIN, and to execute such banking resolutions, signature cards, and related documents as the chosen bank requires.

### 10. Registered Agent

**RESOLVED**, that [NAME / REGISTERED AGENT SERVICE] is confirmed as the Corporation's registered agent at the registered office stated in the Articles of Incorporation.

### 11. Reimbursement of Organizational Expenses

**RESOLVED**, that the Corporation shall reimburse the reasonable expenses incurred in connection with the conversion and organization of the Corporation, including filing fees and professional fees.

### 12. General Authorization

**RESOLVED**, that the officers of the Corporation are, and each of them hereby is, authorized to execute and deliver such further documents and take such further actions as any of them deems necessary or appropriate to carry out the foregoing resolutions.
