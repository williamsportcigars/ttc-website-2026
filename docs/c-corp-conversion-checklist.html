Confirm this is the right path before you start. This checklist assumes a full statutory conversion into an actual Pennsylvania corporation — new entity, new EIN, real stock. If what you actually want is just C-corp tax treatment while staying legally an LLC, that's a single IRS form (8832), not this whole process — ask your accountant which one matches your goal before filing anything below. The full conversion is the right call if you're planning an eventual sale (for the QSBS capital-gains exclusion) or want to issue real stock to investors or employees.
