Why This Show

The Tobacco Center has been part of Williamsport since 1977 — new ownership in 2025 brought new energy without losing the address or the regulars. The YouTube channel (@TheTobaccoCenter) already shows the format travels: individual Shorts have pulled views far beyond the subscriber base, into an audience that's mostly 25–44 — prime cigar-buying demographic.

The podcast extends that same audience into long-form — the format sponsors and listeners actually stick around for.
