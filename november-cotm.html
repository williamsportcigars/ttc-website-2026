November 2026 · The Thanksgiving Feast Collection

This month we're doing something a little different. Privada Cigar Club built three brand-new LCA releases around Thanksgiving dinner itself — Turducken, Pumpkin Spice, and Gravy — so instead of our usual two exclusives, you're getting all three. To round out the table, we're adding two from Asylum — the Schizo Maduro and the Schizo Natural — plus a Privada Farm Rolled Maduro. Six cigars, one table, one holiday. Dig in.

Privada / LCA Exclusive Releases — The Thanksgiving Trio

Turducken 2026

The quintessential Thanksgiving Day cigar — built the way the dish gets built, tobacco stuffed inside tobacco inside tobacco.

Tasting Notes

Sweet earth, cocoa, herbs, roasted nuts, pepper, savory umami.

Best Paired With

Spiced cider, bourbon, mulled wine, or dark holiday ales.

Filler Dominican & Nicaraguan

Pumpkin Spice

Built with Guillermo Peña around the pumpkin spice latte — no infusion, no flavoring, every note naturally occurring in the tobacco.

Tasting Notes

Baking spices, roasted nuts, creamy coffee, a hint of vanilla.

Best Paired With

Pumpkin spice latte, bourbon cream cold brew, oatmeal stout, or spiced pecan pie.

LCA # 156
Vitola Toro, 6 x 50
Wrapper Nicaraguan Jalapa Maduro
Binder Mexican San Andrés
Filler Dominican & Nicaraguan
Strength Full
Age 1 year before release

Gravy

Built with master blender Raul Lanuza to answer one question: what cigar belongs at the end of Thanksgiving dinner?

Tasting Notes

Savory herbs, toasted grains, rich umami, roasted nuts, warm earth with a creamy finish.

Best Paired With

Turkey dinner, buttery chardonnay, brown ale, or leftover pumpkin pie.

LCA # 157
Vitola Robusto Extra, 5½ x 54
Wrapper Nicaraguan Habano
Binder Nicaraguan
Filler Nicaraguan (puro)
Strength Medium to Full
Age 2 years before release

We've never built a Cigar of the Month around a single meal before, but Thanksgiving earned it. Turducken, Pumpkin Spice, and Gravy were made by Privada specifically for this holiday, and we couldn't ship them without a proper table to put them on — so Asylum's Schizo Maduro and Natural and a Privada Farm Rolled Maduro round things out.
