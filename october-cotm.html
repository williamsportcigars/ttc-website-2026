Privada / LCA Exclusive Releases

Coco Berry

Raul Lanuza's experimental Rosé wrapper — a red, sun-grown hybrid that gets full Nicaraguan sun intensity, then a little shade from the plant's top leaves right at the end. A true Nicaraguan puro built to taste like candy.

Tasting Notes

Dark chocolate, espresso, red pepper and sweet molasses. Pre-light freeze-dried blueberry; cold draw of marshmallow cream and berry candy.

Best Paired With

Vanilla cream soda, port wine, blueberry wheat ale, or grilled pork chop with cherry demi-glace.

LCA # 154
Vitola Toro, 6 x 52
Wrapper Rosé Corojo Sun Grown
Binder Jalapa Corojo
Filler Estelí Corojo, Condega & Jalapa

Mexican Chocolate

Built at Tabacalera Annex, the joint factory around AJ Fernandez created to play with tobaccos too scarce for national releases. Wrapped in an extremely dark Mexican San Andrés leaf and box-pressed into a 6 x 54.

Tasting Notes

Sweet molasses, cocoa, red pepper, cedar, and espresso.

Best Paired With

Mocha stout, aged rum, Mexican hot chocolate, or a night in with classic Halloween soundtracks.

LCA # 153
Vitola Box Press, 6 x 54
Wrapper Mexican San Andrés
Binder Estelí, Nicaragua
Filler Estelí, Nicaragua
This Month's Boutique Selections — Crowned Heads / Oz Family

Oz Family Cigars Maracana — Gordo

Tim Ozgener's modern take on the Brazilian-wrapped cigars he helped popularize during his years running CAO — a direct homage to the beloved CAO Brazilia, named for Rio's Estádio do Maracanã.

Tasting Notes

Black pepper, espresso, earth, leather, cocoa nibs, charred oak, and dark raisin sweetness.

Best Paired With

Aged rum, a dark stout, or black coffee.

Vitola Gordo, 6 x 60
Wrapper Brazilian Arapiraca
Strength Full

Oz Family Cigars Karatoba — Robusto Extra

The fourth core-line release from Oz Family Cigars, marking the brand's first partnership with NACSA. Ozgener compares the blend to a hearty, well-seasoned bolognese sauce with a maple-syrup finish.

Tasting Notes

Savory spice, rich and meaty, cedar, coffee, roasted nuts, maple sweetness.

Best Paired With

A dark lager, espresso, or aged rum.

Vitola Robusto Extra, 5½ x 55
Wrapper Ecuadorian Sumatra
Strength Medium-Full

Crowned Heads Broadway — Toro

The first release to carry Crowned Heads' rebranded packaging and hero logo — a tribute to Nashville's lower Broadway and the dark Connecticut Broadleaf wrapper that defines the blend.

Tasting Notes

Dark chocolate, espresso, black cocoa, cream, brown sugar, roasted nuts, cedar, peppery finish.

Best Paired With

Dark rye toast, roasted coffee, or a mid-proof bourbon.

Vitola Toro, 6½ x 54
Wrapper Connecticut Broadleaf (Grade A)
Strength Medium-Full
